# Gangsters 2 visual notes (Crossroad St.)

Source: `Art/Refs/gangsters2-crossroad-st.png` — target look for Lost Empire Levee streets.

## Roads
- **Value:** Light warm gray / beige asphalt — NOT charcoal black. Reads mid-light even in shadow.
- **Texture:** Soft worn grain, subtle patches; no high-contrast cracks dominating.
- **Intersection:** Wide open plaza feel; road is the stage, buildings frame it.
- **Markings:** Minimal; chalk outlines / stains are rare story props, not dense striping.
- **Curbs:** Thin light-gray lip separating road from walk — clear edge, low height.
- **Sidewalks:** Slightly darker than road, cobble/brick tile pattern readable at iso distance.
- **Contrast rule:** Road lighter than walks; walks lighter than building ground floors in shadow. Never pure black pavement.

## Buildings
- **Read as painted iso façades**, not flat roof-only slabs — south/east faces carry the story.
- **Variety per block:** stone civic (pale), brick residential (warm brown), industrial (brick + colored metal roof).
- **Ground floor:** Storefronts, doors, awnings (green cloth pops), bay windows — human-scale detail.
- **Upper floors:** Repeated windows, cornices; roofs can be slate, gable, or blue corrugated metal.
- **Depth:** Soft self-shadow under eaves; buildings cast soft ground shadows onto road.
- **Palette:** Muted period colors — cream stone, brick ochre/brown, occasional saturated awning or roof accent.
- **Avoid:** Uniform gray cubes, identical heights, missing street-level entrances.

## People
- **Scale:** Small vs buildings (~1/4–1/5 storey height); crowd reads as dots of costume color.
- **Silhouette:** Hats, coats, suits — period 1920s–30s readable at distance.
- **Color coding:** Role/identity via suit color (red shooter, blue runner, neutrals for civs).
- **Selection:** Colored square under feet (red/green/blue) — clear, flat, not 3D rings.
- **Motion:** Few units in action (muzzle flash, run pose); most idle/stand.
- **Density:** Sparse intersection — 6–12 visible people, not a packed sim crowd.
- **Vehicles:** Period sedan as props on curb; same scale language as characters (sprite/prop, not hero mesh).

## Lighting & camera (for matching)
- Even daytime fill; soft contact shadows; no black void beyond the block.
- Classic iso (~30–45°); camera frames one intersection as the composition.
- UI sits in bars; world stays readable underneath.

## Lost Empire apply checklist
1. Road albedo mid-light warm gray (boost emissive / lift until it matches this plate).
2. Walks darker textured tile; thin curb strip.
3. Building S/W façades show storefront art; roofs secondary.
4. Characters as small billboard/sprites with foot markers; suit color = role.
5. Keep intersection open — don’t fill the crossroads with props.
