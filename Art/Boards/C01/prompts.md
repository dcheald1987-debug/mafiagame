# C01 boards

Choose one frame of each. Save the PNG and the exact prompt beside it. Modeling uses the Packard side view. The others are for the blockout camera, not for textures.

Style lock, prepended to every prompt:

1931 Saint Lorne, American Great Lakes port, restrained period realism, wet brick, tungsten practicals, no neon noir parody, no modern cars, no modern clothing, photographed, ordinary faces.

## 01 Dock

Night rain, service alley behind a grand 1931 hotel, black Packard sedan parked at a loading dock, one tungsten bulb over a metal door, wet cobbles, bellman's eye height, 35mm.

## 02 Packard

1930 black Packard sedan, side profile, orthographic, neutral grey background, long wheelbase, no people, no text.

## 03 Hill

One-way brick hill street at night in heavy rain, hotel district falling toward industrial lights, viewed from a driver's seat, period cars only, wet asphalt.

## 04 The Cut

Rail trench under a low iron bridge at night, rain, a single car's headlights, freight cars above, no modern infrastructure.

## 05 Green bulb

Small brick garage door on a side street, one green bulb over the door, rain, 1931, no readable sign lettering.
