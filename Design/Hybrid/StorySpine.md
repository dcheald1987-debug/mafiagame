# Lost Empire — Story spine (Levee dock war)

Campaign of weeks on the Levee. No driving chapters.

| Beat | Name | Enter when | Demand / gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Favor owed | Start | Tutorial: Collect S02, Watch S01. Full orders after week 1 Results. |
| 1 | Sal wants the dock | 2 successful weeks + $100 lifetime | Hold S03 (Dock Warehouse A) at week end. |
| 2 | Harlan answers | S03 held for Sal | Survive one more week still holding S03. Harlan (R03) joins the crew. |
| 3 | Dock war | Beat 2 cleared | Own S03 **and** S04 (Hawser Bar) at week end. |
| 4 | Ames watches | Dock war won | Hold S03+S04 with Heat ≤ 2 at week end. |
| 5 | Spine complete | Ames quiet cleared | Brickyard stub already unlocked; districts phase opens next. |
